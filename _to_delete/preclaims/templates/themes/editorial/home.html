03 — The Standard

Four entries in the masthead.

The editorial policy of a reference library: publish the molecule, publish the method, publish the number, publish the date.

A threshold, not an adjective

"High purity" is copywriting. ≥99% by HPLC area, applied as a release gate to every lot, is a specification you can hold us to.

Two instruments, one answer

Chromatography says how much. Mass spectrometry says what. Independent laboratories run both before a compound is catalogued.

Molecular data on the page

Sequence, molecular weight and research category are printed with every listing — the reference detail you'd otherwise go hunting for in a database.
