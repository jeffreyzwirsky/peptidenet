House Standards

A small formulary, kept carefully.

Eighteen compounds, each one handled the same unhurried way — from the release assay to the paper that travels with the vial.

Released at ≥99%

A lot is admitted to the formulary only once it has been measured above the threshold. Nothing is stocked on the strength of a supplier's word.

Read twice, by outside hands

Independent HPLC for purity, mass spectrometry for identity. Two readings, taken by a third party, before anything is put on the shelf.

Lyophilised & sealed

Compounds are freeze-dried to a stable powder and sealed under vacuum. Kept cool, dark and dry, a sealed vial waits patiently for your bench.
