Analytical Standards

Nothing ships on a claim. Everything ships on data.

The release gate

A lot either clears the ≥99% purity threshold or it never reaches the catalogue. There is no second tier and no "close enough" grade.

Two instruments, independently

Reversed-phase HPLC produces the purity figure; mass spectrometry confirms the molecule that figure belongs to. Both are run by third-party laboratories.

The lot file follows the vial

A certificate of analysis tied to the exact lot number on your vial — chromatogram, mass confirmation, appearance and fill weight. Ask for it before you order if you like.
