Where do I actually get legitimate research peptides?+

Right here. A legitimate source is defined by documentation: independent third-party HPLC and mass-spec testing, a batch-matched Certificate of Analysis, and a verifiable ≥99% purity release standard. Every compound in our catalogue meets all three.

Do you provide a Certificate of Analysis?+

Yes. A COA matched to your specific batch ships with every order, and we're happy to send the current batch's COA on request before you purchase. It documents identity, purity by HPLC, and mass confirmation.

Are your products safe for human use?+

No. All compounds are supplied strictly for in-vitro laboratory research and development. They are not drugs or supplements and must not be administered to humans or animals. By ordering, you confirm you are a qualified researcher aged 21 or older.

The vetting criteria

Five things a supplier must be able to prove.

This is the checklist we built the directory around. Run it against us, then run it against whoever else is in your browser tabs.

1 · A stated release threshold

Ours is ≥99% by HPLC area, applied lot by lot. A supplier without a published number has nothing to fail against.

2 · Independent testing

HPLC for purity, mass spectrometry for identity, run outside the seller's own building. In-house-only data is a marketing asset, not a control.

3 · A lot-matched certificate

The COA's lot number must match the vial's. Reused certificates are the single most common failure among the listings we've reviewed.

4 · A published delivery window

{{ site.shipping_window }}, direct from our manufacturing partner, tracked. A real window beats an unverifiable promise of speed every time.

5 · A reachable human

An email address and phone number that answer questions about a batch before you buy — and still answer after you have.
