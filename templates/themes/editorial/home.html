03 — The Standard

Four entries in the masthead.

The editorial policy of a reference library is mostly about restraint: publish what you hold, say where the record stops, and never fill the gap with adjectives.

No analytical file, stated up front

We hold no certificate of analysis for any compound in this catalogue — no purity figure, no identity confirmation, nothing. We will not quote a number we cannot support, and we cannot produce a document on request that does not exist.

Treat the material as uncharacterised

Because nothing has been characterised for us, the only safe assumption is that identity, content and impurity profile are unknown. Any property you need to rely on is yours to establish, in your own laboratory, before you use it.

Reference data on the page

Sequence, molecular weight and research category are printed with every listing as published reference values for the named compound — literature detail about the molecule, not a measurement of the vial you receive.

Delivery, stated plainly
