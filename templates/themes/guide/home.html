What a purity figure actually means

When a supplier states a purity threshold, they are claiming each released batch was measured and met that threshold by a named analytical method, with the identity of the molecule confirmed the molecule's identity. Impurities are the remaining 1% or less.

It's the single most useful number on a certificate — but only alongside the raw chromatogram that produced it. We quote no such number: no batch of ours has been measured, so there is nothing behind a figure we could print. Assume the contents are uncharacterised and characterise them yourself if your work depends on it.

What is a COA and will I get one?+

A Certificate of Analysis is the report a testing lab issues for one specific batch: identity, purity, method and lot number. It's the document this guide tells you to ask every supplier for. You will not get one from us. We hold none, we can't produce one on request, and we publish documentation only where we actually have it.
